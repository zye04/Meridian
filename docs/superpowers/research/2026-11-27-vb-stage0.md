# Visibility buffer stage 0: raw measurements

Result: go (director, 2026-11-27). Sum of `scene early + voxel trees +
scene late + voxel trees late`, ms (the per-pass split bleeds between
timestamps; the sum doesn't). Baseline: mean of 3 runs (spread <= 0.18);
stripped: one or two runs (the vertex strip repeat spread up to 1.3).
Coverage: Mpx covered by cluster triangles + voxels (`--debug-view lod`).
Resolve bound: 0.545 ms per shaded Mpx (view r4: 0.47 ms over 0.863 Mpx of
close leaves) x coverage; it leaves out the resolve's vertex fetch and
barycentric math, and applies the cluster rate to voxel pixels. Projected
net = baseline - both - resolve bound.
